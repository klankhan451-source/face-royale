❓ HOW TO PLAY

  • 🕹️ Drag your left thumb on the left side of the screen — that's your joystick.
  • 🔴 Hold FIRE to shoot. Your fighter auto-aims at the nearest enemy in range.
  • 🔀 SWAP switches between your gun and a melee weapon (🔪 Knife / 🪓 Axe). Get close and hold FIRE to swing!

  • 🟣 Stay inside the purple zone circle — outside it, you take damage!
